## 1. Scouter visual/UX canon

### Physical design
- A **semi-monocle**: single transparent coloured lens over the **left eye**, attached to
  an earpiece unit — a wearable all-purpose computer used by the Frieza Force.
  Japanese name: スカウター (*sukautā*). ([Dragon Ball Wiki](https://dragonball.fandom.com/wiki/Scouter), [Kanzenshuu](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/wiki/Scouter))
- **Lens colours:** **green** is by far the most common (the default in popular imagination);
  **red** (Turles; Bulma wears Raditz's repaired one) and **blue** (Frieza's first-gen unit in
  *DBS: Broly*) are the variant colours; purple appears occasionally. Fan investigation found
  **no canon rule tying lens colour to rank** — colour is aesthetic, so cosmetic colour
  "skins" are canon-safe. ([Kanzenshuu forum](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=5237))

### HUD / readout
- Classic frame: **targeting reticle/brackets lock onto the subject** → alien symbols
  scroll/flicker during the scan with an iconic **beeping** climb → a **large numeric readout
  slams to a stop** on the final power level. Toriyama's stated mechanism is an analog counter
  that rises too fast and breaks — the count-up-then-stop beat is the canonical animation.
  ([CBR](https://www.cbr.com/dragon-ball-z-scouter-explained/))
- The scouter's alien glyphs are a **real 1:1 cipher for English letters**, deciphered by fans
  from *DBS: Broly* frames — digits shown are stylised **Arabic numerals**.
  ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/dragon-ball-super-brolys-mysterious-scouter-language-deciphered-redditor-1401012))
  Design implication: a scan where **our own original glyph set resolves into Arabic numerals**
  is both faithful and legally safer (never reuse Toei's actual glyphs).
- Canon nicety: Bulma reprograms Raditz's scouter to display Earth numerals — in-universe
  justification for an English UI.
- Beyond power reading, canon scouters do distance/target tracking, multi-target readouts
  with directional indicators, and comms (the earpiece) — licence for extra HUD garnish.

### The "it broke!" trope
- Scouters **crack/explode when a reading spikes too high or climbs too fast** — Raditz's at
  Gohan's rage spike (1,307), Vegeta crushing his own in-hand, mass scouter deaths at Kaio-ken
  and SSJ-era powers ("the scouter can no longer measure it" becomes a running theme).
  This is the single most recognisable scouter beat — perfect for the shatter effect.

### "Over 9000" vs "over 8000"
- **Japanese canon: "It's over 8000!"** (8000以上だ). **"Over 9000" is English-dub only**
  (1997 Ocean dub; kept because "nine" fit the mouth flaps better) and became the meme via
  2006-era YouTube remixes. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It%27s_Over_9000!),
  [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/dragon-ball-z-its-over-9000-line-origin-mistranslation/))
- Product note: shatter Easter egg at >9000, with a purist tooltip ("technically 8000").

## 2. Transformation multipliers & reference power levels

### Multipliers — canon vs fanon

Official numbers come from the Daizenshuu guidebooks (esp. Daizenshuu 7, 1996).
([Ultra DB Wiki](https://ultradragonball.fandom.com/wiki/All_Saiyan_Form_Multipliers),
[Kanzenshuu Battle Power Guide](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/battle-power/databook/))

| Form | Multiplier (of base) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Oozaru (Great Ape) | ×10 | **Official** |
| Kaio-ken | ×1.5–×2 baseline; ×3, ×4 (vs Vegeta); ×10, ×20 (vs Frieza — highest canon use) | **Official** |
| Super Saiyan | ×50 | **Official** (validated: 3,000,000 base × 50 = 150,000,000 vs Frieza) |
| SSJ Grade 2 / Grade 3 | ~×75 / ~×100 commonly cited | Fanon — no official number |
| Super Saiyan 2 | ×2 of SSJ = **×100** | **Official** (guidebook relationship) |
| Super Saiyan 3 | ×4 of SSJ2 = **×400** | **Official** (guidebook relationship) |
| Golden Oozaru / SSJ4 (GT) | ×10 of SSJ3 = ×4,000 commonly used | Semi-official / fanon (GT side-canon) |
| Super Saiyan God / Blue | **No official numbers**; god ki is canonically **invisible to scouters** | Fanon |
| Legendary SS (Broly) | "keeps growing endlessly" — no number | Fanon |
| Ultra Instinct | No numbers at all; fan figures span ×100M–×100B | Pure fanon |

**Two-tier design opportunity:** a "Canon" tier (Oozaru ×10, Kaio-ken ×2–×20, SSJ ×50,
SSJ2 ×100, SSJ3 ×400) where every number is defensible, and a "beyond the scouter" tier
where the scouter **canonically errors out** — `ERROR: DIVINE KI NOT DETECTABLE` for
SSG/SSB/UI is faithful, funny, and dodges the fanon-number argument entirely.
Kaio-ken canonically strains the body when overused → maps beautifully onto "PR attempt".

## 3. IP / copyright / trademark risk

### Ownership
Manga © Bird Studio/Shueisha; anime + brand exploitation Toei Animation; games Bandai Namco;
US distribution Crunchyroll (ex-Funimation). Rights management has been in visible flux since
Toriyama's death (2024) between Shueisha, Capsule Corporation Tokyo, and Toei — flux does not
reduce enforcement risk. ([Kanzenshuu](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/translations/weekly-toyo-keizai-article-dragon-ball-rights-dispute/))

### Risk assessment by element

- **Original scouter-inspired design — LOW.** Copyright protects expression, not the idea of
  "an eye HUD with a reticle and a big number". Don't trace the prop, don't reuse frames or
  the deciphered Broly glyphs; design original lens + glyph set. A decade of scouter camera
  apps sitting untouched on official app stores supports this reading.
- **Words "power level", "scouter", "over 9000" — LOW.** Generic/descriptive in context; no
  live trademark found on "over 9000"; merch economy for the phrase runs openly. Don't brand
  the product itself "Scouter™".
- **"Super Saiyan" / the domain supersaiyan.me — MODERATE (biggest exposure).** Toei's US
  "SUPER SAIYAN" mark (USPTO 76088506, filed 2000, toys class) went **DEAD/ABANDONED 2004**
  ([Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/super-saiyan-76088506)) — apparently **no live US
  word mark**, though Japanese registrations certainly exist and famous-mark/UDRP theories
  don't require a US registration. A non-commercial fan/parody site with a prominent
  disclaimer has a real "legitimate noncommercial fair use" UDRP defence, but a domain that
  IS the character-class name could go either way at a panel. **Realistic worst case: lose
  the domain — not damages.** Verify with a direct USPTO TESS search before committing hard
  to the name (sandbox couldn't reach TESS).

### What Toei actually enforces against
They are aggressive, but the pattern is consistent: they hit **(1) actual footage/art/audio
reuse** (DBZ Abridged strikes ended the series; Totally Not Mark lost 150+ videos), **(2)
commercial scale** (bootleg merch C&Ds), **(3) high-visibility projects that threaten
licensing lanes**. They have largely tolerated asset-free non-commercial fan games/tools for
years (Hyper Dragon Ball Z, the scouter camera apps). A free stats-multiplier joke site with
original art sits outside all three. ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/anime/team-four-star-reveal-why-theyre-done-with-dbz-abridged-youtube-series-1324829/), [Kanzenshuu enforcement megathread](https://www.kanzenshuu.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=46953))

### Operating rules (adopted into CLAUDE.md)
1. **Zero copyrighted assets** — no frames, official art, character images, audio rips
   (synthesise our own beep), no traced scouter, original glyph font.
2. **Prominent footer disclaimer** on every page: unofficial fan project, not affiliated with
   or endorsed by Bird Studio/Shueisha, Toei Animation, or Bandai Namco.
3. **Genuinely non-commercial** — no ads, no merch, no paid tier. Money is the single
   biggest risk multiplier.
4. **Frame as parody/homage** in copy (helps fair-use posture and UDRP legitimate interest).
5. **Fold on first contact** — architect so the brand name is swappable; if a C&D arrives,
   rebrand and keep the (far less protectable) scouter-scan concept.
6. **Register a fallback neutral domain** from day one; never sell or monetise the domain.
7. Don't use DBZ terms in app-store listings or paid keywords if those ever happen.

### Saiyan Sans font — usable, with one caveat
Created by Ben Palmer; free for personal **and commercial** use, modification allowed, credit
appreciated not required ([dafont](https://www.dafont.com/saiyan-sans.font)). Caveat: its
design imitates the official DBZ logo lettering — fine for headings/flavour text, avoid for
the actual logo lockup (trade-dress similarity). Verify the licence text in the ZIP.

## 4. Prior art — scouter apps (from the canon pass)

Existing scouter apps are **crowded-but-validating**: "DBZ Scouter", "DBZ Scouter Power
Glasses", "Scouter", "Power Meter Pro" and many more — mostly camera + face-detect apps that
overlay a scouter frame and output a **random** number. **None found combine the scan with
real fitness stats + transformation multipliers** — that's the differentiation. Their long
unbothered existence on official stores is the best real-world evidence for §3's risk read.
Established gag conventions worth keeping: insult readings ("power level of a Saibaman"),
the Vegeta hand-crush as the share-image money shot, "his power level is maximum" (bootleg
translation meme).

Useful canon jokes for the stats engine: farmer = 5 as the average-human anchor; scouters
only read *controlled* ki (explains any silly number); god forms error out; post-Frieza
"readings off the chart".

## 5. Fitness tests & scoring systems

### 5.1 Test catalogue (with rough adult bands + fun record anchors)

**Strength (1RM-based; input kg or lb; normalise as ratio of bodyweight):**

| Test | Untrained | Intermediate | Elite | World record anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bench press | ~0.5× BW | ~1.0× BW | ~1.75× BW | 355 kg (Julius Maddox, raw) |
| Back squat | ~0.75× BW | ~1.5× BW | ~2.5× BW | 490 kg (Ray Williams, raw) |
| Deadlift | ~1.0× BW | ~1.75× BW | ~2.75× BW | 487.5 kg raw PL (501 kg Björnsson, strongman) |
| Overhead press | ~0.4× BW | ~0.75× BW | ~1.0–1.1× BW | BW press = top 5–10% of lifters |
| Weighted pull-up | +0 kg | +34 kg | +93 kg | ([Liftoff standards](https://liftoffrank.com/blog/weighted-pull-up-standards)) |
| Grip (dynamometer) | 40–50 kg avg man | >64 kg excellent | ≥70 kg | ~170–190 kg grip-sport athletes |

(Women's bands roughly 0.5–0.65× the men's ratios; strengthlevel-style tiers ≈ population
percentiles 5/20/50/80/95.)

**Power/speed:** 40-yd dash (rec ~5.5–6.5 s; WR 4.21 s Xavier Worthy), 100 m (rec men
12–14 s; 9.58 s Bolt), vertical jump (men avg 41–51 cm; 45″ Zion combine), broad jump (men
180–215 cm avg; 3.73 m Byron Jones), seated 2 kg med-ball throw (protocol-sensitive — pin
or skip v1).

**Endurance:** VO2 max (avg man 35–40; elite 70–85; record 97.5 Oskar Svendsen), Cooper
12-min run, 5k time ("good" 22:31 M / 26:07 F; WR 12:35 Cheptegei), resting HR (avg ~70;
athletes 40–60; Guinness 27 bpm — invert the scale).

**Bodyweight/reps:** push-ups (avg man 20s: 17–29; 50+ advanced), strict pull-ups (<5% of
Americans manage 10; USMC max 23), plank (2 min = good per McGill; saturate scoring ~5 min —
the 9 h 38 m WR is a stunt).

**Misc fun:** reaction time — Human Benchmark **median 273 ms**, sub-150 ms physiologically
implausible (troll filter); **best run in-browser, it's verifiable** — natural first
mini-game. Punch force: arcade machine 0–999 score (>600 good, 700+ trained) as an optional
novelty input with a "machines vary" disclaimer.

### 5.2 Scoring formulas (all verbatim-sourced, all legally implementable)

- **IPF GL points (2020)** — `Points = Total(kg) × 100 / (A − B·e^(−C·bw))`; coefficients
  verified against [OpenPowerlifting's calculator source](https://gitlab.com/openpowerlifting/ipf-points-calculator)
  (e.g. M raw SBD: A=1199.72839, B=1025.18162, C=0.009210; M raw bench: 320.98041,
  281.40258, 0.01008; F raw SBD: 610.32796, 1045.59282, 0.03048; F raw bench: 142.40398,
  442.52671, 0.04724). **~100 ≈ world-record level — a ready-made 0–100 scale.**
- **DOTS** — `Total × 500 / (A·bw⁴ + B·bw³ + C·bw² + D·bw + E)`; M: A=−0.0000010930,
  B=0.0007391293, C=−0.1918759221, D=24.0900756, E=−307.75076; F: −0.0000010706,
  0.0005158568, −0.1126655495, 13.6175032, −57.96288. (~500 ≈ world class.)
- **Wilks** (classic + 2.0) — full coefficient sets captured in the research pass; DOTS/IPF
  GL preferred for new work.
- **Cooper VO2 max** — `VO2max = (distance_m − 504.9) / 44.73` (±10%).
- **Resting-HR VO2 max (Uth 2004)** — `VO2max = 15.3 × (HRmax/HRrest)`,
  `HRmax ≈ 208 − 0.7 × age` (Tanaka) — endurance estimate with zero equipment.
- **1RM from rep maxes** (accept "100 kg × 5" input): Epley `w(1 + r/30)`; Brzycki
  `w/(1.0278 − 0.0278r)` (best 1–10 reps); Wathan `100w/(48.8 + 53.8·e^(−0.075r))`
  (best 10–20). Reject r > ~15.
- **Age adjustment** — powerlifting's Foster (14–23: ×1.23→×1.00) and **McCulloch** (40+:
  ×1.000 at 40 → ×1.511 at 66 → ×2.190 at 83) coefficients, [official USAPL table](https://www.usapowerlifting.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/USAPL-Age-Coefficients.pdf).
  Endurance: WMA age-grading (% of age/sex world best, the parkrun model) — another
  ready-made "% of world record" scale.
- **Sex adjustment** — native to Wilks/DOTS/IPF GL; separate norm tables elsewhere.

### 5.3 Composite "battle power" design (synthesis of AFT / CrossFit / IPF models)

- Precedents: US Army **AFT** sums five 0–100 events to 500 with per-event floors (public
  US-gov [scoring tables PDF](https://www.army.mil/e2/downloads/rv7/aft/AFT_Scoring_Scales_250601.pdf) — freely usable); CrossFit-gym level calculators frame the
  composite as a weakness-finder; per-lift band interpolation → 0–1000 is the
  strength-calculator pattern; IPF GL / parkrun age-grading = "% of world class".
- **Design rules that make it fair AND fun:**
  1. Normalise every test to a common 0–100 (percentile or %-of-elite) before combining —
     never average raw units.
  2. **Category sub-scores** (Strength / Power / Endurance / Skill) as the scouter's radar
     readout; power level = mean of *category* means, so 4 strength tests + 1 run doesn't
     strength-weight the result.
  3. **More stats = more accuracy, not more points** — show a rising "scan confidence %";
     mean not sum (the #1 fairness trap).
  4. Gentle breadth bonus (×1.0→×1.15 for covering all categories) — AFT's no-dump-stats
     spirit without hard floors.
  5. Honest 0–100 core + **display-layer blow-up**: shown power level ≈
     `9000 × (score/100)³`-ish so elite users break 9000. Transformation multipliers are
     pure display sugar on top of the honest layer.
  6. Bodyweight normalisation for strength only; speed/endurance self-penalise.

### 5.4 Practical input notes

- **Self-testable, no gym:** push-ups, pull-ups, plank, 5k (phone GPS), Cooper, RHR,
  broad jump, sprints (hand-timing flatters ~0.2–0.3 s), **reaction time in-browser**.
- **Gym:** the barbell 1RMs (self-report on trust — the whole strengthlevel model does),
  grip dynamometer (~£20), vertical jump (wall + chalk).
- **Special:** true VO2 max — accept wearable estimate with disclaimer, or derive via
  Cooper/RHR.
- **Troll caps** (reject / "are you sure?"): bench 360 / squat 500 / deadlift 510 / OHP 260
  kg; bodyweight 30–250 kg; grip 200 kg; vert 130 cm; broad 380 cm; 100 m 9.5–60 s; 40 yd
  4.1–15 s; 5k 12:30–90:00; VO2 15–100; RHR 25–120; push-ups 200; pull-ups 50; plank ≤1 h
  (score saturates ~5 min); reaction ≥100 ms. Cross-field incoherence (300 kg bench + 0
  push-ups) → confidence penalty with "scouter malfunction?" copy.
- **Units:** store metric internally; convert at UI (lb ×2.20462); locale-default imperial
  for US; mm:ss input masks for times. Wilks/DOTS/IPF GL are kg-defined — convert first.

### 5.5 Licensing flags

- **strengthlevel.com: DO NOT scrape/copy** — T&Cs prohibit derivative calculators/standards.
  Use the percentile-band *concept* with independently sourced thresholds.
- **OpenPowerlifting**: open-licence bulk data + visible calculator source — the legitimate
  route to derive our own strength percentiles.
- IPF GL / Wilks / DOTS / McCulloch coefficient tables, Cooper/Uth/1RM equations: published
  formulas, safe to implement. Army AFT tables: US-gov public document, free to reuse.
- Topend Sports / Marathon Handbook norm tables: editorial — paraphrase with attribution.
  Human Benchmark: cite the public stats page, don't scrape.

## 6. Tech research (prior art gap, imaging, effects, stack, hosting, privacy)

### Prior-art gap confirmed
Web-based prior art is only static meme templates ([Imgflip "Vegeta breaking scouter"](https://imgflip.com/memegenerator/254515329/Vegeta-breaking-scouter-over-9000)) and generic
overlay editors; the scouter-HUD-on-your-photo experience lives entirely in ~2011–2017
ad-laden mobile apps (static overlay + random number). **None have**: animated scan sequence,
configurable colour/style, stat toggles, transformation multipliers, or a shatter payoff.
A polished, no-install, no-account animated web version is a genuine gap — polish is the
differentiator, and the shatter beat is the shareable money shot.

### Client-side image pipeline (recommended architecture)
- **Live view:** photo as `<img>` with the HUD as absolutely-positioned **SVG/DOM on top**
  — all animation is normal CSS/JS, crisp and cheap to iterate.
- **Export:** draw photo + a static HUD frame onto an offscreen **Canvas 2D** →
  `canvas.toBlob('image/jpeg', 0.9)`. Since the HUD is our own SVG, rasterise it via
  `new Image()` + `drawImage` (or redraw primitives in canvas). **No DOM-screenshot library
  needed**; if ever required, `html-to-image` (~11 kB, maintained) — avoid `html2canvas`
  (maintenance mode, chokes on modern CSS). `satori` is an OG-image generator, not a
  screenshot tool.
- **EXIF orientation:** solved in modern browsers — `createImageBitmap(file,
  {imageOrientation: 'from-image'})` honours EXIF; browser `<img>` decode is auto-corrected.
  Canvas re-encode at export **strips all EXIF including GPS** — a privacy feature worth
  stating.
- **HEIC (iPhone):** set `accept="image/jpeg,image/png,image/webp"` and iOS Safari
  **auto-transcodes HEIC → JPEG** at the picker ([Apple forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/743049)).
  Fallback for stray HEIC files: magic-byte sniff (`ftypheic` at offset 4) → lazy-load
  [`heic2any`](https://github.com/alexcorvi/heic2any) (MIT, **~2.7 MB — never in main bundle**).
- **Download/share:** primary mobile CTA = **Web Share API Level 2 with files**
  (`navigator.canShare({files})` gate; iOS 15+, Chrome Android; Firefox desktop lacks it) —
  this is how the image reaches WhatsApp/Instagram/Photos. `<a download>` fallback (note:
  iOS saves to Files, not Photos — lead with Share on iOS). User-gesture + HTTPS required.
- **Wow-factor option:** lazy-loaded **MediaPipe Face Detector** (`@mediapipe/tasks-vision`,
  Apache-2.0, wasm, fully client-side) to auto-lock the reticle onto the face; draggable
  manual placement as fallback. (face-api.js is abandoned; MediaPipe is current.)

### Effects
- **Scan sweep / warning flicker / glitch: pure CSS** — gradient bar `translateY` keyframes +
  `mix-blend-mode`, `steps()` flicker, clip-path RGB-split glitch. Zero bundle cost.
  Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Shatter (the payoff): Canvas 2D Voronoi shards** — ~30–80 seeds densest at a crack
  origin, [`d3-delaunay`](https://d3js.org/d3-delaunay/voronoi) (ISC, ~10 kB gz) for the
  polygons, clip the composite into shards, animate velocity/rotation/gravity in rAF, white
  crack-line flash first. ~200 lines own code; best looks-per-kilobyte. (WebGL shader
  version prettier but more work; PixiJS = whole engine for one 2-s effect — overkill.)
- **Sequencing: GSAP 3.13+** — **now 100% free including all formerly-paid plugins** (post
  Webflow acquisition, April 2025; core ~23–27 kB gz) — ideal timeline orchestration for
  scan → calculate → reveal → shatter. Alternative: Motion's mini `animate` (~5 kB) if we
  stay minimal.

### Stack + hosting
- **Vite+React SPA vs Next 16 `output: 'export'`:** either produces a pure static bundle;
  familiarity favours Next (prerendered shell, metadata API for OG tags, file routing for
  future mini-games) with **none** of the server-feature ceremony under `output: 'export'`.
  Vite is the lighter toolchain if preferred. TypeScript + Tailwind v4 either way.
- **Hosting winner: Cloudflare Workers static assets** — **unlimited bandwidth** (the only
  free tier that survives a Reddit/viral spike), free custom domain, 100k Worker
  invocations/day for the optional OG endpoint. Note: **Cloudflare Pages is legacy** — new
  projects go on Workers static assets ([migration docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/migration-guides/migrate-from-pages/)).
  Netlify free is now credit-based (300/mo; bandwidth 20 credits/GB) — punishes viral
  traffic, and K already manages Netlify credits carefully; don't add another property.
  Vercel Hobby is non-commercial-only with 100 GB/mo; GitHub Pages has no edge functions.
- **OG/social cards:** crawlers can never see the client-side composite. **v1: static
  branded OG card** ("What's YOUR power level?") — the user's actual image travels as a
  file via Web Share anyway. **v1.1: parameterised OG endpoint** — one Cloudflare Worker
  with [`workers-og`](https://github.com/kvnang/workers-og) (satori + resvg-wasm) rendering
  "Power level: 9,001 — SUPER SAIYAN" from query params (`/?pl=9001&form=ssj`) — personalised
  unfurls, zero photo storage, GDPR-clean. Uploading composites for OG: **ruled out**.

### Privacy / GDPR
- Photo never leaves the device → no processing by the operator, no Art. 6 basis needed;
  say it proudly ("your photo never leaves your device — we can't see it").
- **localStorage for user's own stats/prefs = strictly-necessary/functional exemption**
  under ePrivacy Art. 5(3) — no consent banner, provided it's genuinely functional and
  disclosed. Never stash a tracking UUID "for later" — purpose kills the exemption.
- **Analytics later without a banner:** Plausible (EU, cookieless) or GoatCounter (free) —
  legitimate-interest, no-identifier counting. **GA4 would need a banner — don't.**
- Net: **zero consent banner** for v1 and after adding privacy-friendly analytics. Ship a
  short `/privacy` page anyway (transparency + marketing).

### Bundle budget
First-load JS beyond framework: **well under 50 kB gz** (GSAP core + d3-delaunay);
heic2any (~2.7 MB) and MediaPipe (MBs) strictly lazy-loaded on demand. Hosting cost £0.

## 7. Power-level formula bank (researched 2026-08-10)

Every published, defensible way to turn self-reportable inputs into watts/joules/kcal
or a composite. The killer insight: **"power level" can be literal WATTS** — real physics,
real formulas, and real humans genuinely produce four-to-five-digit numbers.

### 7.1 Jump power (vertical jump + body mass → watts)
- **Sayers (1999) — the modern default:** `Peak W = 60.7×jump(cm) + 45.3×mass(kg) − 2055`
  (r≈0.91 vs force plate). 80 kg + 45 cm jump → **4,301 W**; NBA-level 86 cm → **6,790 W**.
- Harman (1991): `Peak W = 61.9×jump + 36.0×mass + 1822`; also mean-power variant.
- Johnson & Bahamonde (1996) adds standing height (countermovement):
  `Peak W = 78.6×VJ + 60.3×mass − 15.3×height(cm) − 1308`.
- **Lewis (1974)** underestimates ~2× — perfect as a joke "OLD SCOUTER FIRMWARE" mode.
- Sources: [Sayers paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10211854/), [Topend calculator](https://www.topendsports.com/testing/vertical-jump-power.htm)

### 7.2 Cycling / rowing
- **FTP direct entry** — already watts. Coggan W/kg ladder = ready-made tier list:
  untrained 1.5–2.5 → recreational 3.3–3.7 → Cat 1 ~5.0 → World Tour 5.8–6.4+ (Pogačar ~7.1).
- **Concept2 rowing:** `Watts = 2.80 / pace³` (pace = split-seconds/500). 2:00 split → 203 W.
- **Wingate 30 s:** college male mean peak **699.5 W** (9.18 W/kg); >11 W/kg elite.

### 7.3 Running
- **ECOR model:** `P(W) ≈ 1.04 × mass(kg) × speed(m/s)` flat-ground (Stryd-adjacent).
  80 kg @ 5:00/km → 277 W. Full version adds `0.5×ρ×CdA×v³` drag.
- **ACSM textbook:** `VO2 = 0.2×speed + 0.9×speed×grade + 3.5` (m/min) → chain to watts.

### 7.4 Strength → energy (pure physics, CSCS-exam grade)
- `Work(J) = mass × 9.81 × ROM(m)`; `Power(W) = Work / rep seconds`.
  100 kg bench × 0.40 m ROM = **392 J/rep**; 0.8 s concentric → ~490 W.
  Hardcode ROMs: bench ~0.40 m, squat ~0.55–0.65 m, deadlift ~0.50 m (scale by height).
- VBT anchor: optimum mean propulsive power at bar speed ≈ 1.0 m/s →
  shortcut `P ≈ load × 9.81 × 1.0` at ~40–70% 1RM.

### 7.5 Punch (the silly-wattage printer)
- `E = ½ × m_eff × v²`; `P = E / contact time`. Fist+forearm ~2.5–4 kg, elite hand speed
  9–11 m/s, contact ~10–30 ms → 4 kg @ 10 m/s = 200 J → **10,000 W instantaneous**.
- Force benchmarks: untrained jab ~1,000 N; pro cross ~4,500 N; elite heavyweight >6,000 N.
- Arcade punch machines: no standard unit, vibes-only input. PowerKube "Franklin" unit
  proprietary — don't clone.

### 7.6 Metabolic (the body as a power plant)
- **Mifflin–St Jeor BMR:** `10×W + 6.25×H(cm) − 5×age + 5` (men) / `− 161` (women).
- **1 kcal/day = 0.04843 W** → BMR 1,700 kcal = **~82 W idle draw** ("you idle like a
  lightbulb"). TDEE multipliers ×1.2–×1.9.
- VO2→watts: 1 L O2/min ≈ 349 W *metabolic*; ×~20–25% gross efficiency for mechanical.
  1 MET = 1.162 W/kg metabolic.

### 7.7 Composite architecture
- **RAS (Relative Athletic Score) methodology is public and cloneable:** percentile-rank
  each metric vs a population, score 0–10, average, re-normalise. This IS our composite
  layer's architecture (validates the PLAN §3 design).
- **SPARQ is proprietary** (never published; only community rSPARQ approximations) — use
  "SPARQ-style" language only, never the name as a formula claim.
